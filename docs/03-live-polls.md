# Live polls

The opening gallup is a **ten-minute quick-fire pulse check**. Questions and answers should be short enough to understand at a glance. The presenter does not invite discussion between questions.

The live-voting application supports anonymous single-choice questions with 2–5 options. Participants can change a vote while a question is open. Closed questions cannot reopen. One optional free-text feedback question, limited to 160 characters, must be last.

## Timing model

- 0:00–1:30 — show QR code and let people join;
- 1:30–7:30 — seven questions, approximately 45–50 seconds each;
- 7:30–10:00 — summarize at most three patterns and move directly to the demo.

Do not read every percentage. The results form a profile of this room, not a scientific survey.

## Two-minute synthesis

The presenter should complete these three sentences from the visible results:

> Tässä huoneessa on ______ kokemusta.  
> AI on ______ osa arkea.  
> Käyttö, koettu hyöty ja luottamus ovat ______ suhteessa toisiinsa.

Then transition:

> “Katsotaan nyt yhtä ohjelmistoa, jota käytitte juuri itse.”

Do not ask individuals to justify answers during the gallup. Save stories and disagreement for the optional discussion after the formal hour.

## Demo poll — outside the opening gallup

Use only if the project history is known and the reveal can be truthful.

**Question**  
Ihminen vai AI?

**Options**

1. Ihminen
2. AI
3. Molemmat
4. En tiedä

The reveal should explain why this binary is insufficient. Show the brief, constraints, review, tests, and ongoing ownership.

## Closing poll

Create a duplicate of the opening mood question:

**Question**  
AI-fiilis?

**Options**

1. Innostunut
2. Utelias
3. Ristiriitainen
4. Huolestunut
5. Väsynyt

Compare the largest categories only. This is not a controlled pre/post measurement. A more conflicted answer can mean the presentation succeeded in adding nuance.

## Final feedback question — must be last

**Prompt**  
Mikä väite tai kysymys jäi pyörimään mieleen?

The comment wall opens the optional discussion after the formal hour.

## Application order

1. Experience
2. Recent use
3. Perceived speed
4. Verification
5. Autonomy
6. Output quality or one optional replacement
7. Opening mood
8. Demo origin — optional
9. Closing mood
10. Final feedback

Create every required question before the session. The final feedback item must remain last.

## Facilitation rules

- Keep the question screen clean and read the question once.
- Close voting as soon as the distribution stabilizes; do not wait for every attendee.
- Do not discuss individual answers during the quick-fire sequence.
- Polls describe this room at this moment. Never generalize the percentages beyond it.
- Avoid forcing personal disclosure about unemployment or workplace policy.
- Keep the session code visible during the join period.
- Have a hand-vote fallback for experience, recent use, and closing mood.
